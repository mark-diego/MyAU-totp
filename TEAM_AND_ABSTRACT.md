## Abstract

MyAU is a web-based student profile and records portal for Africa University. It gives students a place to maintain their profiles and organize projects, skills, achievements, certificates, clubs, and recommendation requests. University staff can review submissions, maintain official results, and manage student-related workflows according to their assigned roles. The system uses Supabase for authentication and data storage, with row-level security to limit access to authorized users. Sign-in uses authenticator-app multi-factor authentication. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, MyAU aims to make student information easier to maintain and university review tasks more consistent and secure.
